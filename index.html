OxyBrush 3D

A high-performance 3D sculpting tool engineered for seamless workflow. Features multiresolution subdivision handling up to 5.2M triangles, rich screen-space ambient/cavity shading, 16 versatile brushes, procedural alphas, and advanced masking capabilities. Now available on Flathub.

OxyPixel 2D

A blazing-fast, robust 2D pixel-art editor. Powered by the efficiency of Rust, relying on the custom oxypixel-core engine and the highly responsive egui framework.
